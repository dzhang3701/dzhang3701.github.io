What am I looking at?

The model scores all 50,257 tokens in its vocabulary. Softmax converts those logits into probabilities. Temperature changes their sharpness; top-k and top-p remove candidates and renormalize the remaining probabilities.

To sample, we draw a uniform random number u between 0 and 1. We arrange tokens from most to least probable and add their probabilities until the cumulative total passes u. That token is appended, and the model runs again on the new context.

The interval above preserves the distribution used for the last draw. The table shows the distribution for the next token. Clicking a table row picks a token manually, without a random draw.
